Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2025

1. Introduction

Telegram Restricted Content Downloader (“TRCD”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a Chrome extension that allows users to manage and download files from Telegram channels or chats that they have legitimate access to. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this policy.

2. Information We Collect

TRCD itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) to our servers. The extension processes data locally within your browser.

  • Google OAuth data: If you sign in with your Google account, TRCD only requests basic profile information (name, email, profile picture) to verify identity and sync settings. This data is handled through Google’s secure OAuth 2.0 system and is never shared or stored by us.
  • Telegram data: Files, messages, and content accessed via TRCD remain within Telegram’s infrastructure and your local browser storage. We do not collect or host these files.
  • Usage statistics: We may use anonymous, aggregated metrics (e.g., feature usage counts) to improve user experience. No personal identifiers are included.

3. How We Use Information

Any data processed by TRCD is used solely for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Authenticating users via Google OAuth (if applicable).
  • Providing and improving extension functionality.
  • Ensuring secure and authorized access to Telegram content.

4. Data Storage and Security

TRCD does not maintain external servers or databases. All user data and downloaded content are stored locally on your device, under the protection of your browser and Telegram’s encryption mechanisms.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to any third party. We may disclose information only if required by law, or to protect our legal rights.

6. Third-Party Services

TRCD interacts with external platforms (e.g., Telegram, Google OAuth) under their respective terms and privacy policies. Please refer to:

  • Telegram Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

7. Children’s Privacy

TRCD is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data processed through Google OAuth. Such requests can be made directly via your Google account settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
